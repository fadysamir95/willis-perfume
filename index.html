GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order?

Choose a fragrance from the collection, add it to your cart, then press "Checkout via WhatsApp" — or message us directly on WhatsApp and we'll help you choose your size.

How much does delivery cost?

Shipping is 35 EGP inside Cairo and 50 EGP to other governorates. It's free for orders above 1,000 EGP.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery, Vodafone Cash or InstaPay — just tell us your preferred method in the WhatsApp order message.

Can I exchange or return my order?

Yes — you can exchange or return any item within 24 hours of receiving it. Just message us on WhatsApp.

Are the fragrances original?

Yes — our fragrances are inspired by iconic scents and made with high-quality oils for a consistent, long-lasting performance.

When will my order ship?

Orders are shipped within 24 hours, and delivery takes 2–5 working days depending on your governorate.
